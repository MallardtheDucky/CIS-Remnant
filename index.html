The Continuance

DESIGNATION: CIS REMNANT (EXTERNAL) · CAPITAL SYSTEM: OSSERITON
one world hidden is survivable
One world hidden is survivable. An empire is detectable.

The Confederacy of Independent Systems never legally ceased to exist. When the Separatist Council died on Mustafar in 19 BBY, the surviving provincial authorities under Admiral Vexis moved to Osseriton and kept the government running. Five centuries on, that emergency administration is a permanent state. We are not a successor state or a revival movement. We are the continuation.

Government

PROVINCIAL CHARTER OF CONFEDERATE CONTINUANCE

The Charter descends from CIS provincial statutes and sets up a Provincial Administration with separate civil and military branches. The military holds final authority during declared security emergencies. This is not dictatorship. The state believes that if Osseriton is revealed, outside powers will destroy it immediately. This is the Permanent Emergency doctrine.

The Brotherhood of Osseriton

INTERNAL NAME: THE ORDER OF THE VIGIL · ALIAS: "THE QUIET BROTHERS" (VEIL DESIGNATION) · GOVERNED BY THE COVENANT OF THE VIGIL

A deliberately tiny order of Force-sensitives, never more than one hundred, serving the Authority as its blades, spies and hidden hand. Not Jedi, not Sith, not a religion. A discipline, a monastic rule engineered to do two jobs: kill quietly and fight openly, and never be traced home. It rules no territory and governs no one but itself.

Origin

Founded, not inherited. The first sensitive, a Jedi survivor, was smuggled to Osseriton by the Veil Network during the Imperial Purge era. Others trickled in over decades. In 30 ABY, Vexis, who cannot feel the Force and distrusts it, and Vayle drafted the Covenant of the Vigil, binding the fractious survivors into one sworn order. Vayle was named first Magister.

The balance between the hands rests on one lifetime post.

Bone triangles on a black-oxblood field. A crimson bar marks the Chapter and the Magister. Every rank bears the Order's mark, a pale hexagon holding a red slit-pupil eye. The Seneschal is a lateral post standing beside the Marshal.

OBLATE → ASPIRANT → NOVICE → SWORN → PRIOR → PRECEPTOR → MARSHAL → MAGISTER

Advancement. Every advancement needs time served, an aptitude shown and a sponsor of higher rank who vouches for the candidate to the Chapter. The Chapter votes, and the Magister ratifies and may veto any name without explanation. Because the Measure caps the Order at one hundred, a brother rises only when someone above him dies, leaves, or the Order admits fewer than it loses.

THE HALLS
A decade of training

Sparring, covercraft, the Creed and the Counsel-archive. Most never finish, and washing out is honorable.

A Sworn may take a second oath binding him to a Calling. Rank gives authority and Calling gives purpose. The Edge and Shroud are the real powers, and the Eye supports both.

The Edge · 4–6 brothers

Function: saber and Force combat. Duels enemy Force-users.

Flavor: proud and martial, the Order's war-blades.

  • The Marshal is always drawn from the Edge.
  • Called in for what cannot be avoided, and a cell's one Edge-brother is its answer when a fight cannot be dodged.
  • The Order trains for open fighting while its doctrine forbids it, so every Edge-brother carries that contradiction.
The Shroud · 6–8 brothers

Function: infiltration, covert work, assassination.

Flavor: patient, invisible, cold.

  • Shroud operatives sit in a dozen systems, known in none.
  • Cover identities are mandatory and no witnesses and no bodies is the standard.
  • Two or three Shroud get a cell in, and the Quiet is theirs to keep.
The Eye · 2–3 brothers

Function: Force sensing, scholarship, the Counsel-archives.

Flavor: watchful, strange.

  • Supports both other Callings and keeps the archive of what the Order has learned.
  • The smallest Calling and the least seen, even within the Order.

Mixed cells

Cells are mixed by design: two or three Shroud to get in, one Edge for what cannot be avoided. Each Prior leads a cell of two to four Sworn, and every Sworn works beside a droid Counsel.
